Questions

Is anything I type saved?

No. Every number is worked out on your device and is never sent or saved. It is cleared when you close the page.

How is EMI calculated?

EMI uses the standard reducing-balance formula with monthly interest, the same way banks calculate home, car and personal loan EMIs.

How do I remove GST from a price?

Choose "Including GST", type the price and the rate. The tool shows the price before GST and the tax part, split into CGST and SGST.
